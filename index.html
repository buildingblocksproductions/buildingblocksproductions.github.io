Synopsis

A father and his terminally ill daughter set out on a cross-country trip to find the boy she loves. Along the way, they have to face their shared grief and work out what it means to hold on and to let go.

Written and directed by Scout Taylor-Compton, inspired by a loss of her own. Filmed in Tulsa, Oklahoma. The first feature from Building Blocks Productions.

Leadership

Writer · Director · Co-founder

Scout Taylor-Compton

Best known as Laurie Strode in Rob Zombie’s Halloween and Halloween II. She made her feature directing debut with Bring the Law, and wrote and directed Road to Recovery and Survive: The Game.

Producer · Writer · Co-founder

Producer of Road to Recovery and Survive: The Game, and writer of One More Day, now in development. Tom also brings a background in stunts and performance on Obi-Wan Kenobi, Ahsoka and The Mandalorian.
